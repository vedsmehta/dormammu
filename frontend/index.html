How it works

It finds the deals. You approve. It closes them.

  1. 01

    It learns your profile

    Student, veteran, senior, new in town, or none of these. Your profile decides which discounts it asks for and what proof a vendor will want.

  2. 02

    It finds the best deals

    One request sends out every call at once. Each call asks for the rates you qualify for, so you see prices most people never get quoted.

  3. 03

    You choose

    Options come back ranked, with and without your discount. Nothing is signed, booked or canceled until you approve it.

  4. 04

    It executes and negotiates

    It calls back to lock in what you picked, and pushes on the price using our negotiation harness.

What it handles

  • Compare quotes

    Internet, renters insurance, movers. Several vendors called in parallel and compared side by side, with and without your discount.

  • Book appointments

    Doctors, dentists and repair shops that only book by phone. Dormammu checks when you are free and locks in a time.

  • Negotiate and cancel

    Calls to ask for a better rate, or to cancel the service you stopped using months ago.

Nobody picks up? Dormammu carries the same conversation over text instead of leaving a "call us back".

The negotiation harness

It negotiates the way the research says works.

  • Reaches the right desk

    It gets to the retention team, where the discounts are, instead of general customer service.

  • Asks for your rate first

    The student, veteran or senior rate is asked for on every call, before any deal is closed.

  • Asks for the best price, then waits

    It asks what the lowest available rate is and stays quiet. That silence costs nothing and moves numbers.

  • Stays honest

    It only cites real advertised prices. It never invents a competing offer, a hardship or a problem with the service.

  • Knows when to stop

    Two escalations at most. Any offer that comes with conditions is brought back to you, not accepted.

The harness is a step-by-step playbook built from published research on bill negotiation and tested before it calls for you.

The ledger

It remembers every account, so you don't have to.

Each call writes down what was agreed: the vendor, the rate, when the promo ends, when it renews. That list is yours, in one place.

When a date comes up, Dormammu asks first. You say yes, it makes the call.
